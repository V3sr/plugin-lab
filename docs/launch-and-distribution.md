# Distribution framework — product selection pending

Updated: 2026-09-29. GuildBrief-specific positioning, demos, outreach, launch dates and acquisition targets are withdrawn. The [historical launch draft](archive/guildbrief/launch-and-distribution.md) is inactive. No advertising or outreach has been sent.

## Conditional sequence

1. Select one remaining product and a narrowly defined recurring task.
2. Recruit relevant opt-in discovery participants; investigate a recent real problem and its current workaround.
3. Validate an authorized data route and the exact product's distribution eligibility.
4. Build a synthetic demonstration, then a bounded working prototype with meaningful quality and access checks.
5. Run consenting pilots and observe voluntary repeat tasks.
6. Publish reproducible setup and useful demonstrations on permitted routes.
7. Repeat the acquisition channel that produces retained users. Consider paid promotion only after setup and repeat-use evidence exist.

These stages are a framework, not scheduled work or approval to contact anyone, install on their systems, post ads or spend money.

## Candidate-specific channels and demo angles

| Candidate | First cohort | Useful demonstration | Potential channel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Course author QA | Course authors and instructional designers | Find a real structural defect, inspect the proposed diff and preview the fix | Practical course-author tutorials, approved professional pilots |
| Creator experiment ledger | Gaming/animation creators | Compare the planned creative experiment with actual outcome and production spend | Creator walkthroughs and reusable experiment templates |
| Receipt/return evidence | Student buyers | Show a supported action deadline, evidence and an uncertain exception | Purchase-organization tutorials and opt-in consumer pilots |
| Permission regression kit | MCP maintainers | Reproduce an access failure and show the regression check catching it | GitHub examples and detailed technical articles |

Edit advertising to match implemented behavior. Label synthetic examples. Do not invent novelty, endorsements, users or measured savings. Product-specific copy and outreach should be drafted after a replacement is selected.

## Measurement

Track qualified visits, setup completion, first useful task, voluntary repeat task and mature retention cohorts. Define users, organizations and their denominators separately. Track quality, latency, cost and support effort with disclosed minimal telemetry. Do not equate an integration's reachable audience with active users.

Set numerical targets and time windows before each experiment once the selected workflow's cadence is understood. The previous GuildBrief targets are not transferred to another product.

## Budget and publication
