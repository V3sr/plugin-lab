# Product discovery interview guide

Updated: 2026-09-29. This guide covers the remaining candidate workflows. The former organizer/event-handoff interview plan is withdrawn.

## Recruit by task

Talk to people who recently performed the candidate's task: course authors, creators, buyers or MCP maintainers. Include users outside the founder's immediate circle. Establish consent and keep private notes and documents out of the public repository.

## Questions

1. Walk me through the last time you did this task.
2. What was hardest or most error-prone? Show the workaround using material you are allowed to share.
3. What consequence did that cause?
4. How often do you need to repeat it?
5. Which tool or person solves it now?
6. What would make changing your workflow worthwhile?
7. Which input data can you authorize, and which must be excluded?
8. Who can approve setup or integration access?
9. Would you use this in an assistant, a standalone app or your existing tool? Why?

After those questions, show a synthetic example of the candidate. Ask the participant to complete a task, find an error and explain what action the result supports. A compliment is weaker evidence than bringing a second task voluntarily.

## Record privately

Recent problem, frequency, workaround, consequence, desired outcome, data constraints, installation authority, preferred interface, concrete trial commitment and permission for follow-up. Publish only authorized aggregate findings. No interviews are claimed completed.
